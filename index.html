Analytical Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

1. Defining Pressure: Match Pressure Index (MPI)

Every legal ball is assigned a continuous score $ ext{MPI} \in [0, 100]$ combining four contextual signals:

  • Scoring Rate Stress (42%): In 2nd innings, normalized Required Run Rate ($ ext{RRR} > 7.5$). In 1st innings, deficit below venue-adjusted par trajectory.
  • Wicket Scarcity (33%): Non-linear penalty $((10 - W_{ ext{hand}})/10)^{1.6} imes 100$, capturing exponential collapse risk after the 5th wicket.
  • Phase Leverage (25%): Death overs (16–20) weighted $1.0 imes$, Powerplay collapses $0.75 imes$, middle consolidation $0.65 imes$.
  • Climax Closeness Bonus (+18 pts): Triggered in the final 24 balls when runs needed are within $2.2 imes$ balls remaining.

2. Fair Comparisons & Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

To prevent small-sample flukes (e.g., one 15-ball cameo), we enforce strict statistical safeguards:

  • Minimum Exposure Gate: $\ge 120$ high-pressure balls ($ ext{MPI} \ge 65$) faced or bowled across IPL & T20Is (2022–2026).
  • Empirical Bayes Shrinkage: Observed HP Strike Rate / Economy is shrunk toward the league HP prior with pseudo-count $k = 150$ balls:
    SR_adj = (n * SR_hp + 150 * SR_league) / (n + 150)
  • Baseline Delta ($\Delta$): Directly measures whether a player improves or degrades under pressure vs their own non-pressure baseline ($ ext{MPI} < 65$).

3. Composite Rating Formulas (0–100 Scale)

Clutch Batting Rating (CBR):
0.35·Z(Adj_HP_SR) + 0.25·Z(SR_Delta) + 0.20·Z(HP_Avg) + 0.10·Z(Boundary%) - 0.10·Z(Dot%)
Clutch Bowling Rating (CBwR):
-0.35·Z(Adj_HP_Econ) - 0.25·Z(Econ_Delta) - 0.20·Z(HP_Bowl_SR) + 0.20·Z(HP_Dot%)
